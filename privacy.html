Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

QuickAI is a short-form video channel and website based in Brisbane, Australia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and what you can do about it. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), even where the Act may not strictly apply to a small business.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you sign up for the weekly "5 free AI tools" email.
  • Where you came from: a short code (for example "yt", "tt" or "ig") showing whether you reached this page from YouTube, TikTok or Instagram. It is stored with your signup and added to links you click.
  • Basic technical data that our web host and email provider log automatically, such as IP address, browser type and time of visit, and whether you open or click our emails.

We don't ask for your name, phone number, address or payment details, and we don't knowingly collect sensitive information. You can visit the site without giving us any personal information.

Why we collect it (APP 3, APP 6)

  • To send you the weekly email you asked for.
  • To understand which platforms and tools people find useful, so we can make better videos.
  • To keep the site and mailing list secure and working.

We only use your information for these purposes, or for related purposes you'd reasonably expect, or where the law requires it.

Email and unsubscribing

We only email people who have signed up. Every email identifies QuickAI and has an unsubscribe link, in line with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth). Unsubscribe requests are actioned within 5 working days.

Who we share it with (APP 6, APP 8)

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We use a few service providers to run the site:

  • Newsletter provider: Kit (ConvertKit LLC, kit.com), our email newsletter provider, stores the email list and sends our emails.
  • Website host: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) serves this page and keeps standard server logs.

These providers may store data outside Australia, including in the United States and the European Union. We choose providers that commit to protecting personal information, but overseas laws may differ from Australian law. By signing up, you agree to your information being handled this way.

Affiliate links and third-party sites

Some links on this site are affiliate links. When you click one, the tool's website (or its affiliate network, such as PartnerStack or Amazon) may set cookies to record that you came from QuickAI so we can earn a commission. We don't receive your personal details from them, only aggregated reports such as clicks and sign-ups. Outbound links also carry tags like utm_source so the other site can see which platform you came from. Once you leave our site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't use advertising cookies. It stores the platform code ("src") in your browser's session storage until you close the tab.

Keeping it secure (APP 11)

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including using reputable providers and strong account security. We delete or de-identify information we no longer need, for example when you unsubscribe and ask us to delete your details.

Accessing or correcting your information (APP 12, APP 13)

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us at oki_grok@outlook.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Complaints

If you're worried about how we've handled your information, contact us first at oki_grok@outlook.com and we'll try to fix it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has signed up, tell us and we'll remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
